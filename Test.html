Machinery & Production Capabilities

Our state-of-the-art facility is fully equipped with advanced industrial packaging and blending systems to ensure uniform quality, high volume capacity, and strict hygiene compliance.

Industrial Powder Mixers

Heavy-duty stainless steel mixers with 270kg / 500kg capacity, engineered to achieve homogenised dry premix blends with exact batch formulation consistency.

Bulk Powder Storage Silo

Hygienic enclosed storage silo featuring a 2 Ton tank capacity designed for bulk raw material handling and seamless gravity-fed transfer into primary packaging lines.

Automated Sachet Line

Multi-lane vertical form-fill-seal system dedicated to high-speed, precise single-serve sachet packaging and tight heat sealing.

450g Pouch Packaging

Vertical pouch-filling line optimized for medium pack retail bags, integrated with screw conveyor elevators for accurate dispensing.

900g Pouch Packaging

High-capacity packaging system engineered for precise volumetric dispensing of 900g jumbo family size and commercial pouches.

Automated Film Cutter

Automated high-precision film slitting and cutting station ensuring exact precision trim dimensions and seamless outer packaging setup.

Thermal Shrink Wrapping

Thermal heat tunnel bundler designed for outer bundle shrink-wrapping of product boxes into sturdy secondary packaging for transport.

Continuous Inkjet Printer

Integrated inline coding system for high-speed printing of expiry dates and batch numbers to satisfy full quality compliance.
